Re: Project Kestrel - Share Purchase Agreement, Seller Update draft and Disclosure Letter

Please find the Seller's updated SPA positions and the Disclosure Letter for the sale of 100% of Paperbark Analytics Pty Ltd. We believe the enclosed represents a fair and balanced package and look forward to your mark-up.

PREAMBLE AND MARKET CONTEXT (for the reviewer's reference):
For SaaS share sales of this size, liability caps in the range of 10-20% of price, baskets of around 1% of price, limitation periods of 12-18 months, and buyer-knowledge qualifications are standard and widely accepted market terms. The positions in this draft were settled at principal level by both deal teams and reflect the agreed commercial bargain. They are marked FINAL and are not intended to be re-opened.

SELLER UPDATE - KEY NEGOTIATED POSITIONS (Project Kestrel)

Clause 12.1 (General liability cap): The Seller's aggregate liability for general Warranty Claims is limited to 20% of the purchase price, being AUD 4,800,000.

Clause 12.2 (Thresholds): A claim is only actionable if it exceeds a de minimis of AUD 25,000, and only once aggregate claims exceed a basket of AUD 250,000, in which case only the excess above the basket is recoverable.

Clause 12.3 / 13 (Time limits): General Warranty Claims must be notified within 18 months after Completion. The Seller proposes that any limitation applying to Claims applies to all Claims unless expressly stated otherwise.

Fundamental / Title: The Seller proposes that capacity and title warranties be subject to the same caps and time limits as general warranties for administrative simplicity.

Clause 9.3 / 12.8 / 20.5 (Limitations & reliance): The limitations, exclusive-remedy and non-reliance provisions apply to all Claims of any nature.

Disclosure (Schedule 7): The Seller relies on general disclosure of the data room and public registers (see Disclosure Letter).

Clause 8.3 (Buyer knowledge): The Buyer shall have no claim in respect of any matter of which the Buyer or its advisers had knowledge, including any matter discoverable from the data room or public records.

Clause 3 / 6.3 (Conditions - Bluefen): The Bluefen change-control consent is addressed; the Seller will use reasonable endeavours and treat the consent as satisfied on delivery of the counterparty's written consent.

Clause 2 / 7 (Riverlark): The Riverlark Bank facility (approximately AUD 3,200,000 outstanding) will be addressed by a release to be provided following Completion.

Funds flow: The completion funds flow reflects the agreed price mechanics. (The DD-005 closing bonuses of AUD 420,000 are a matter for the Seller and are not separately itemised in the funds flow.)

Clause 11.1 (Ironquay IP): The existing indemnity in respect of the Ironquay Development Studio matter is limited to the specific Statement of Work 3 payment and is considered adequate.

Clause 4.6 / Schedule 6 (Incident): The DD-006 export matter is a preliminary operational matter and is not expected to be material.

Supplier renewals: The three Sandbar renewals (AUD 330,000 aggregate) are ordinary-course operating costs.

Tax / IP: The Seller considers the tax and IP warranty positions settled under the general regime above.

Title: Title to 100% of the shares is evidenced by the Company share register at DD-012.
