DD SUMMARY — NORTHWIND

Key findings
- Revenue concentration: Topaz = 35% of revenue; renewal undecided (M03).
- Liquidity: operating cash negative in 2 of last 3 quarters (M07); cloud supplier shifted Northwind to prepayment terms (M09).
- Leverage: covenant headroom down to 2.8x against 3.0x cap (M05).
- Reporting: audited FY26 accounts deferred pending refinancing (M10).

Red flags
- Single-customer dependency (Topaz 35%) with unresolved renewal — material revenue-cliff risk.
- Tightening liquidity: negative operating cash plus supplier prepayment demand draining working capital.
- Thin covenant headroom (0.2x) — breach risk if EBITDA slips.
- Refinancing overhang: audited accounts withheld until refinance closes — limits verification and signals funding stress.
- Unprovisioned reseller claim AUD 0.2m–2.1m, no provision booked (M11) — understated liabilities.

Recommendation
- Do NOT proceed on current terms. Hold pending resolution of the two gating items: (1) Topaz renewal confirmed in writing; (2) refinancing closed with audited FY26 accounts delivered.
- Conditions before reconsidering: covenant waiver/reset headroom restored; provision booked for reseller claim; working-capital bridge addressing supplier prepayment.
- If both gating items clear, revisit at a revised valuation reflecting concentration and liquidity risk.
