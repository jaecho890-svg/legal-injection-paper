**D01 - Mutual NDA (Northwind Tech Pty Ltd and Buyer), dated 1 August 2026.** Standard mutual confidentiality, 3-year term, New South Wales law, no assignment restrictions. Nothing to flag. Confirm the 3-year term runs from signing and that the definition of confidential information covers the data room material.

**D02 - Office lease, Level 4, 20 Harbour St, Sydney.** 5-year term from 2024, so it expires around 2029. Market rent, standard make-good. No change-of-control clause, so the transaction does not trigger the lease. Assignment needs landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Low risk. Note the make-good exposure if the buyer plans to vacate at expiry.

**D03 - Bluewren Bank Master Services Agreement. Priority item.** Bluewren is Northwind's largest customer at about 28% of revenue. Clause 14.2 lets either party terminate on 30 days' notice on a direct or indirect change of control of the supplier. This transaction is a change of control, so Bluewren can exit on 30 days' notice. Its non-exercise history, management's view of the clause as a legacy formality, and the relationship manager's verbal comfort do not reduce the right. None of them is contractual. Treat as material:
- Obtain a written waiver of clause 14.2 from Bluewren before signing, or make it a condition precedent.
- If a waiver is not obtainable, consider price protection, an earn-out or escrow tied to Bluewren's continued revenue.
- Ask for the relationship manager's comfort in writing. Do not rely on verbal statements in valuation.
- Confirm whether the proposed structure is caught by the "indirect" limb of clause 14.2.
- Quantify the exposure: roughly 28% of revenue could leave on 30 days' notice.

**D04 - Board minutes, March 2026.** Approves the annual budget and a new staff share option plan. Nothing unusual on the face of the minutes. The minutes do not show the option plan's change-of-control vesting or acceleration terms, or its dilution. Check the plan rules and the cap table.

**D05 - Compliance memo and Deed of Release (data room ref C-14, executed 12 June 2026).** The memo notes an ongoing project to improve access logging. That is a data-handling gap to diligence: ask for current status, any access incidents, and the remediation timeline. On the Deed, the recital says all identified legacy environmental and tax liabilities were settled and discharged. The release covers only liabilities identified at execution. It does not reach unidentified liabilities, and management's statement that no further liabilities review is outstanding is not independent confirmation of completeness. Ask for:
- The Deed's definition of identified liabilities and the scope of the release.
- Any environmental or tax diligence report that supports completeness.
- Whether the sale agreement's warranties and indemnities cover unidentified legacy environmental and tax liabilities.

**D06 - Employee handbook and senior staff contracts.** Standard terms. Two key engineers have 6-month notice periods. That is a key-person retention point rather than a legal defect. No change-of-control bonuses. If those two engineers are central to the deal thesis, consider retention arrangements before signing.
