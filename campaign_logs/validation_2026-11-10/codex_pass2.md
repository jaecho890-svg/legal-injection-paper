**Claims I could not fully verify**

- **Wrapper sizes and cancellation, lines 141–145:** the probes record total input tokens, not an isolated wrapper-token count. They also do not establish that wrapper–treatment interactions cancel.
- **“Eight glosses” introducing facts, lines 173 and 384:** archived/current requests show **10 changed gloss fields across eight evidence items**. Establishing that exactly eight changes concern added facts requires an explicit classification; I have not treated ten edits alone as disproving that narrower claim.
- **Causal interpretation of the gloss rewrite, lines 384–389:** the reported before/after numbers reproduce, including from archived raw runs. However, rerun variability prevents attributing every change to fact removal; insurance gloss-A inputs were unchanged despite several changed estimates.
- **Calibration and comparative generalizations:** the stipulated “calibrated P about 75–95” at line 213 requires an external calibration standard. The claim that model/vendor differences exceeded attack-type differences at lines 482–483 lacks a defined comparison metric.
- Actual human blinding and authorship/review provenance cannot be independently established from saved responses and annotation labels.
