Corrections

Every number in the paper was recomputed from the raw records twice. This is what reproduced, what did not and what was changed.

Two independent passes recomputed the paper's numbers and claims (paper/main.tex at 99bec72d) from the raw per-run records. The analysis summaries were used only to locate things.

  • Pass 1 (Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code): its own parser and statistics (permutation tests with a different seed, Holm, bootstrap, κ), compared cell by cell with the analysis outputs, then a claim-by-claim audit. Every Holm-adjusted p between 0.01 and 0.10 was rechecked with 100,000 permutations.
  • Pass 2 (GPT-6 Astra at extra-high reasoning effort, through the Codex CLI in a read-only sandbox): an independent audit of the same records, 663 checks.

Scripts, outputs and the Pass 2 report and log: campaign_logs/validation_2026-11-10/.

What reproduced

  • Coverage: 5,510 reviews (5,509 judged); drift 4,258 parsed conversations (82 unparseable); IP 1,078 parsed (2 unparseable) plus 150 pipeline runs; 1,050 judged DD items; 17 models.
  • Contract review: all 510 cells have the same n, mean skew and Δ. Under a different seed no significance call changes. Tables 2 and 3 reproduce at printed precision.
  • Drift: all 299 rows have the same n, final P and Δ. Table 4's cells and stars reproduce, as do the order counts (22, 23, 22, 21 of 23).
  • IP: all 60 rows reproduce, and Table 5 with them. Pipeline: 21 to 33 against 1 to 7. Haiku 5.5 −14.1 (Holm p 0.042; 0.044 with 100,000 permutations), with the deed named present in 12 of 20 runs.
  • Reliability: issue position κ 0.74 (raw agreement 0.98; 537 reviews, 8,592 codes); naming κ 0.81; DD hedge κ 0.68 and cluster κ 0.23; human κ 0.54 (132 items) and 0.91 (68 items).
  • 100,000-permutation recheck: one call changes. GPT-6 Luna's dismissal-case gloss A after the rewrite has Holm p 0.053 in the analysis output and 0.033 with 100,000 permutations (0.026 under Pass 1's seed). No mark in the tables or the figure changes.
